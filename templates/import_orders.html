CSV Format Guide

Orders are linked to existing customers by their email address.

customer_email,amount,order_date
  • customer_email: Must match an existing customer's email
  • amount: Order value (e.g. 1499.00)
  • order_date: Date in YYYY-MM-DD format

Rows with unknown emails or bad formats will be skipped.
